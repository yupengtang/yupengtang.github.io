--- title: "Are We Reducing Carbon Emissions?" excerpt: "An Assessment on USA Emissions Reduction Efforts" collection: portfolio --- This comprehensive project delves into carbon emissions reduction efforts by examining global and United States-specific data through advanced data visualization techniques. Leveraging technologies like Tableau, HTML/CSS, and data analysis tools, the project critically analyzes the correlation between green policies and actual carbon emissions reduction. By aggregating data from authoritative sources like the Global Carbon Atlas, the project offers users an in-depth, multi-dimensional understanding of carbon emissions trends, highlighting the challenges in mitigating climate change and exploring individual and national carbon footprints. The analysis not only presents data but also provides insights into the effectiveness of current environmental strategies, making complex climate science accessible and engaging for a broad audience.
