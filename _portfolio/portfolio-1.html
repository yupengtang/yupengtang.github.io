--- title: "Are We Reducing Carbon Emissions?" excerpt: "An Assessment on US Emissions Reduction Efforts
" collection: portfolio --- This project analyzes carbon emissions reduction efforts, focusing on the United States and global trends. Using interactive visualizations like choropleth maps and line charts, we explore the relationship between green policies and emissions reduction. Technologies like Tableau help visualize data from sources like the Global Carbon Atlas, allowing users to explore personal and global carbon footprints.
