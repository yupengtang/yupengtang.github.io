I recently graduated from Georgia Tech with dual M.S. degrees in Computer Science and Computational Science & Engineering. My work focuses on foundation model reliability, multimodal AI, and ML systems. I study model behavior and build the infrastructure needed to evaluate and deploy models at scale.

I study how large language models and vision-language models use evidence, revise decisions, and represent uncertainty across language, vision, and real-world signals. I also work on post-training, including reward hacking under verifiable rewards and calibrated abstention for frozen multimodal world models. In parallel, I build GPU-optimized inference pipelines, large-scale evaluation platforms, and production-grade retrieval and serving systems. I am especially interested in problems at the intersection of frontier modeling and systems engineering, including reliable multimodal inference, evidence-grounded generation, scalable evaluation, and efficient deployment under real-world constraints.

I am a Research Intern at Stanford's CogT Lab, working on LLM and VLM agents for multimodal reasoning and human-AI collaboration. I currently work as a Robotics Engineer at Pudu Robotics, engineering and deploying enterprise robotic systems in dynamic environments, with a focus on autonomous navigation, human-robot interaction, system integration, and performance optimization. I also contribute upstream bug fixes and regression coverage to open-source ML systems including PyTorch/TorchRL, Hugging Face Transformers and Accelerate, Liger-Kernel, and Axolotl.
