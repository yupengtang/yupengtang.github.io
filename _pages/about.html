About me

I recently graduated from Georgia Tech with dual M.S. degrees in Computer Science and Computational Science & Engineering. My work focuses on foundation model reliability, multimodal AI, and ML systems. I study how large language models, vision-language models, and multimodal agents use evidence, revise decisions, and handle uncertainty, with an emphasis on post-training and reliable multimodal inference. Alongside this research, I build GPU-optimized inference pipelines, large-scale evaluation infrastructure, and retrieval and serving systems to evaluate and deploy models under real-world constraints.

I am currently a Research Intern at Stanford's CogT Lab, where I work on LLM and VLM agents for multimodal reasoning and human-AI collaboration. In industry, I work as a Robotics Engineer at Pudu Robotics, engineering autonomous robotic systems for dynamic real-world environments, with a focus on navigation, human-robot interaction, system integration, and reliability.

I contribute upstream fixes and regression coverage to open-source ML systems including Hugging Face Transformers, timm, and Accelerate, as well as PyTorch TorchRL, Google ADK and gemma.cpp, LinkedIn Liger-Kernel, and Axolotl. Previously, I was a Machine Learning Engineer Intern at GMI Cloud, where I worked on production multimodal generation systems, including inference optimization and serving for a 12B diffusion transformer on H100 GPUs, video super-resolution for text-to-video pipelines, and diffusion-based virtual try-on.
