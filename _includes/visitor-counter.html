About these counts

Visitors are unique visits estimated from network and browser information, without cookies: repeat views of a page in the same session count once. “This page” is the same measure for the page you are on. Totals start on {{ site.visitor_counter.since | date: '%B %-d, %Y' }}; earlier traffic is not included. Figures refresh every few hours, and browser privacy settings and blockers may affect them.

Powered by GoatCounter, an open-source analytics service. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences are respected. If the service cannot be reached, the last figures your browser received are shown.
