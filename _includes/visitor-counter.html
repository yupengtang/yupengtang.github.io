About these counts

Views count every load of a page on this site. Visitors are estimated from network and browser information and counted once per day, so someone who returns on another day is counted again. Only a salted hash is kept to tell visitors apart, for at most two days; no IP address is stored and no cookie is set. Totals start on {{ site.visitor_counter.since | date: '%B %-d, %Y' }}; earlier traffic is not included. Automated traffic is excluded where it can be recognized, and browser privacy settings and blockers may affect these numbers.

Counted by this site’s own service on Cloudflare Workers, with no third-party analytics. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, your visit is not counted. If the service cannot be reached, the last figures your browser received are shown.
