A 400+ student undergraduate Linear Algebra course. Recognized as **Outstanding Teaching Assistant** at the end of the academic year.

The course covered vector spaces, matrix factorizations (LU, QR, eigendecomposition), orthogonality, determinants, and applications in engineering contexts.

## Responsibilities

Graded 600+ handwritten problem sets each week with written feedback focused on identifying conceptual gaps, particularly around eigendecomposition, linear transformations, and systems of equations.

Maintained attendance records and coordinated assignment tracking across multiple sections. Held weekly online office hours and fielded 30+ student questions per week on topics from row reduction to inner product spaces.
